Paint over the head in each facing. The grid is 32 by 32 world pixels centred on the head; the figure beneath is the game's bald body, so you see what you cover. P pencil, E eraser, D dropper (picks from your layer, else from the body), right-click erases, hold Shift to paint a line from the last pixel. Save writes <style>.json; put it in data/hair/ and add the name to data/hair/index.json, then in the game __game.setLook({ hairStyle: "<style>" }).
