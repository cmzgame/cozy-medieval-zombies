# Art round 13: the castle battlement, wholly outside the walkway

The brief as given to the artist agent, 2026-10-29 (read `../ROUND-RULES.md` first). Output: `battlements/` (`gen.py` and its modules `core.py`, `geom.py`, `pieces.py`, `scene.py`, `out/` with 40 pictures and 16-bit depth maps, `preview.png`, `README.md`, `manifest.json`). Approved by Michael the same day and wired in `#574` (the depth maps are not read: depth is planar). The reference was an illustration of "Medieval castle battlements" (a wall walk, its parapet, merlons, and machicolations) that is not in the repo.

Michael, 2026-10-29: "design me a short stone wall with these merlons or parapets or whatever the heck they are. A stereotypical castle battlement, and make sure the whole of the battlement sits outside the walkway, as in hanging over, not intruding on the walkway. In CSS terms, I want margin not padding on the battlements box model lol."

**How it fits the game:** walls stand on tile edges (`n` and `w`), 96 px tall; a castle wall's top is a walkway, a floor tile on layer 1 (96 px above the ground). The battlement is the parapet on the walkway's outside edge: a short wall piece on that storey's edge with the walkway's floor on ONE side and none on the other, its stone wholly on the outside of the edge line, so the walkway tile keeps its whole diamond.

**The camera problem, solved by four pictures:** the camera looks from the south-east. For a walkway whose outer edge is its north (`n`) or west (`w`) edge the battlement is on the far side: the camera sees the merlons' inner faces and the world through the crenels (FAR). For one whose outer edge is south or east (the `n` or `w` edge of the next tile) it is on the near side: the camera sees the outer faces, a string course, and corbels, with the walkway behind (NEAR). They are not mirrors: each is rendered from the real solid with the wall's light. Deliver `north_far`, `north_near`, `west_far`, `west_near`, each with 3 plain variants and a rare arrow-loop variant, `late`/`early` cuts for an edge ending in an inner corner; corners (`outer_n/s/e/w`, `inner_n/s/e/w`); run ends (`end_<E|S|W|N>_<far|near>`).

**Proportions and period:** parapet 28 px high (53% of the 53 px figure), merlons 44 px (16 wide, 16 thick, 16 above the parapet, a cube like the reference), crenels 16 wide with their floor at 28; a true head-height merlon (about 55) would hide the player behind a near one. One merlon and one crenel per 32 px edge segment, merlons centred on the vertices, so a run of any length composes with no phase data. Stone: the village's `wall_stone` palette and 8-high courses.

**Preview:** a castle block (far battlement on the north and west runs, near on the south and east), a jogged wall walk with an inner corner and a free end, figures on the walkway behind the near battlement and in front of the far, at 1x and 3x, with 6x close-ups of a merlon and crenel on each straight piece. State what the engine needs.

**Weak points the artist reported:** on the far sides the lower wall hides the overhang; the near overhang's shadow is painted on the wall below, so a battlement over a walkway with no stone wall under it shows a dark smear; the variants differ only in joints and a crack; 40 pictures is a lot to wire (the minimum is the 4 plain straight pieces and the 4 outer corners).
