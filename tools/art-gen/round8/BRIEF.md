## What a stair is in the world (docs/49 section 4, read it whole)

- A straight run of **three tiles** climbing **one storey, 96 px**, in any of the four grid directions; each tile rises 32 px (one "crate"). A body walking it rises evenly, 32 px a tile, and steps off the top onto the **landing**, the tile beyond the third step on the floor above (planks, part of the upper floor, not part of this picture).
- **Its sides are closed**: nobody steps off sideways. Above it the upper floor has a stairwell (no floor over the three tiles).
- Materials: **wood** (planks, the house stairs) and **stone** (cellars and towers later). Wood matters first.
- It stands against a wall or free in a room (the tall house's runs along a partition). Bodies stand on its treads: the figure's feet must sit on the tread under it.
