# Art round 12: the castle gates, 2 and 3 tiles wide, with a portcullis

The brief as given to the artist agent, 2026-10-29 (read `../ROUND-RULES.md` first). Output: `gates/` (`gen.py`, `out/`, `preview.png`, `README.md`, `manifest.json`, draft sprite sheets). Approved by Michael the same day and wired in `#570`. The reference photograph (a pointed round arch in rough grey stone, an iron portcullis of flat bars with spiked points along its bottom edge lowered in the arch, the courtyard and a far wall plainly visible through it) is not in the repo.

Michael, 2026-10-29: "can you build me a castle gate that is 2 tiles wide and a castle gate that is 3 full tiles wide, each for just the gap, you need walls on either side obviously to make an arch, both with gate art for a 3 frame animation: closed, middle, raised but still visible. And make sure the gate is properly see through? See inspiration photo. This would be in the stone wall stone, not the cliff stone. ... I am trying to fill a couple of holes for this castle I want to make."

**Deliver, for the width 2 and the width 3 (two definitions):**
1. **The gate's own picture, the opening only:** the part that fills the gap in the wall; the wall either side is the ordinary `wall_stone`, so the picture must join it with no seam (same block size, joints, outline, cap, the 96 px wall height; read `docs/52` and `data/sprites/wall_stone.json`). A gate occupies 2 or 3 consecutive edge segments, so its picture spans 64 or 96 px of the run, drawn for both the `n` and `w` runs (the engine mirrors nothing). An ARCH in the wall: jambs at both ends, a voussoir arch (a keystone), the wall continuing above to the 96 px cap; the opening tall and wide enough for the 55 px figure to walk through with margin. Structure like art round 9's openings: the arch/frame is the wall part, the portcullis the animated leaf, so the engine animates the leaf alone.
2. **The portcullis:** three frames of the leaf: closed (lowered to the ground, filling the arch from sill to crown), middle (about half raised), raised (lifted nearly into the arch, still VISIBLE: its lowest crossbars and spiked points hang just below the crown). Iron: dark, hard pixels, flat bars 2 to 3 px wide in a grid (verticals every 6 to 8 px, crossbars every 10 to 12), each vertical ending in a spike at the bottom edge, chunky at 1x. **See-through:** the gaps between the bars are alpha 0, so the ground and a figure behind show through the closed gate; no solid dark fill behind the bars. Draw the passage's reveal (the jambs' inside faces, the arch's underside) as a darker stone tone for depth, but never an opaque panel across the opening.
3. **Preview:** a stone wall run with the 2-wide gate and a figure at it in all three frames, then the 3-wide, for both runs, at 1x and 3x, ground (grass and paving) visible through it; a line per gate with its three frames side by side. Draft the JSON sheets and write in the README what engine support they need.

**Result and decisions (kept for the next gate):** openings are 48 px (2 wide) and 80 px (3 wide), not 56 and 88: the 8 px voussoir ring needs an 8 px pier. One middle frame, not two (a second would make the swing smoother). A portcullis is see-through even closed but blocks bodies: Michael's rulings, in `docs/02` 2026-10-29. A 1 wide gate (a tall narrow arched door) and plain arches 1, 2, and 3 wide with no gate are asked for in issue 571.
