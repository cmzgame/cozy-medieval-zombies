One item per file. Pick the figure (the dead have their own rest pose; a layer remembers which body it was painted on), the slot, name it, pick a part or leave auto (the part under the cursor), and paint on the full frame in each facing. Substitution parts (torso, legs, the body) only take pixels where the generated part already is, so the standard silhouette holds; free parts (arms, hands, feet, head) take anything and follow their joint, arms turning with the arm. Pixels are saved relative to each part's anchor, marked in red. P pencil, E eraser, D dropper, F fill, right-click erases, Shift paints a line from the last pixel. Save to game writes data/layers/<slot>/<name>.json (hair under data/hair/) and lists it; in the game, Settings has a row per slot to try a layer on, and Reload picks up new saves. To bind a layer to an item for good, give the item "art": { ..., "painted": "<name>" } in data/items.json.
