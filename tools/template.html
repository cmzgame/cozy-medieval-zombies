Character template exporter

The rest-pose body at all eight facings, 4x, for the artist to draw over (docs/25). One cell is one facing: 48 x 72 game pixels at 4x = 192 x 288, four cells across and two down, in the game's facing order s, sw, w, nw on the top row and n, ne, e, se below. Each part's anchor (red cross) and segment (a line from its anchor to its other end) is marked; the JSON gives the same numbers in 1x frame pixels.
Parts tint: torso, legs, arms, head. Anchors: torso at the neck (segment neck to hip), each leg at its hip (hip to foot), each arm at its shoulder (shoulder to hand), the head at its centre. The hand and foot are accent areas at the ends of the arm and leg segments. The body drawn here is the generator's placeholder with no undergarment (the goobers are asexual, docs/25 Q1); the artist's body replaces it.
