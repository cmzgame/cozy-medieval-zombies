# Art 06: Furniture, fixtures, pumpkins

**Status: in the game** (2026-10-11). Files in `data/art/village/`. Plumbing is `history/17-village-art-build.md`. Written 2026-10-12.

All furniture is **outlined all the way round** (`#2b2522`) because the player uses it. Long sides run along world Y (`/` on screen); one drawing covers both orientations by mirroring, except where noted. Anchors are the tile's top vertex at ground level of the footprint's top tile (`anchors_furniture.json`).

Furniture wood: tops `#7d5a3c`/`#77553a`, left faces `#6a4a30`/`#644529`, right faces `#4f3622`/`#4a321f`, plank gaps `#4a3322` `#3a2716` `#2c1d12`. Metal `#2b2a32` with `#4a4952` highlight. Stone matches the stone wall.

**Slicing 2-tile pieces:** the footprint is world X 0 to 32 by Y 0 to 64 from the anchor, and the tile boundary is the line from world (0, 32) to (32, 32). The engine cuts a two-tile piece along a **vertical screen line** at the second tile's centre, moved 9 px toward the first tile (`sliceOf`, `SLICE_INSET`), after the first tile-boundary cut let far walls draw over the bed's quilt and the chimney breast.

**Mirroring costs:** a mirrored bed, table or fireplace swaps its lit side. Accepted (`17` ruling 6). The chair and pumpkin are drawn separately where it would matter more.

## Fire frames and the fireplace

The firebox is drawn **empty and dark**, and the three flame frames are separate unlit sprites that the engine animates, lights and draws over the firebox. The engine owns the radius and flicker (`fire: radius 3, room, burns, cooks, maxFuel 4`); the art owns only the picture of the flame. Never bake a glow into the flames or the hearthstone.

## Pumpkins and jack-o'-lanterns

Files: `pumpkin_0` (32 x 29) and `pumpkin_1` (26 x 25, smaller and rounder), plain; `jackolantern_left` and `jackolantern_right` (32 x 29, the face turned each way) with three glow frames each, `jackolantern_<left|right>_glow_0..2`, the same size and drawn at the same position as the body.

- **Seen from the game's 2:1 angle so the top shows.** `pumpkin2.py` samples a 3D pumpkin (radius 10, half height 8, eight ribs) lit from the top-left and rasterises it, replacing a first flat version that read as a side view.
- **Tones:** deep `#6e2e12`, shade `#9c4419`, base `#c2601f`, light `#d77e33`, highlight `#e8a052`, groove `#86391a`; stem `#3a2716` `#5e4028` `#7d5a3c` with a cut top `#a88a62`; dark cuts `#1c120c`.
- **The jack-o'-lanterns follow the fireplace's rule:** an *unlit* body with dark empty cuts, plus three *unlit* glow frames that fill the cuts (`#b8401c` `#e07a26` `#f2b33e` `#f8dc7a`). The engine supplies the light and the flicker; no halo. Two faces are **drawn separately**, not mirrored, so the light stays top-left.
- **Anchors are the ground point under the pumpkin's centre**, the engine's tile-centre convention (`ground_anchor_px`: `pumpkin_0` (16, 23), `pumpkin_1` (13, 21)). No top-vertex conversion.
- **Outlined**: they are game objects (gatherables, carriable). The engine gives the jack-o'-lantern a fire (`radius 1.5, lit, flicker`) and glows it by drawing `flame_<n>` over `idle_<n>`. October 2026 is where it came from, and the plain pumpkins are the same pumpkin the crops draw at stage 2.

## Open items

- **Chest, barrel, campfire, torch post, lanterns:** still generated by the engine; drawn art welcome in this style (outlined, 2:1 iso, unlit).
- **More chair facings:** the two drawings plus mirrors give four. A stool, a bench, a bookcase, a stove and a bar counter are natural next pieces, each needing the same footprint-and-mirror decisions.
- **Bed variants:** the facings with the headboard toward the camera are not drawn until wanted.
- **Interactable highlight:** the engine draws the outline for gatherables, but furniture's outline is baked into the art. Changing the outline colour means redrawing, not a shader setting.
