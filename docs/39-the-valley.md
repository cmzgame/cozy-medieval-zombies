## 0. How to read it

- **Tags.** [built]: in the game on `main`. [change: how]: built, and this scope changes it. [to build]: in scope, not built. [open #N]: not settled; the issue holds the question. Two tags on one line mean both (a thing to build whose details are open).
- **Unlocks.** "the kit": a new character starts with it. "found": in a placed container or a loot table, named. "made at X": a recipe at that station. "Skill N": a hard gate from the skills trees (#275): below the level it cannot be equipped, cast, made, gathered, or planted, and its hover card says what is needed ("Needs Melee 4"). Level 1 is what every new character has. "a teacher": learned from a villager who teaches the skill. "a region": comes from beyond the valley, named only.
- **Recipes are learned and earned** (`docs/02`, 2026-10-18): from a skill's level, a teacher, or a region's reward [change: every recipe is known from the start today]. A new character knows every level-1 recipe of every skill and nothing else; a level's perks come by themselves, and its recipes and unlocks are learned once from the skill's teacher after reaching it (ruled 2026-10-23); recipe drops from bosses, a scrap of paper consumed on use, are reserved for later (Michael).
- **Multiplayer:** every entry is built for a guest in the same commit (`CLAUDE.md`, the first rule of shipping); section 14 lists only what is multiplayer's own.

## 2. Buildings and what a player can build

### Structure

- **Wall lanterns** [built]: 33 on the valley's walls, each a point of light (5.12), put out and lit again (2026-10-25, `43`).
- **Rooms** [built]: found by flood fill; a shed, hut, or forest cabin is one room, a house at least two; no roofs, one storey.
- **A log wall** [built]: a log, with a hatchet carried.
- **A door** [built]: three planks, with a smith's hammer; stamped over a wall, of the wall's material; starts closed; blocks sight and bodies.
- **A window with shutters** [built]: two planks, with a smith's hammer; shutters open and close; closed, they block sight; nobody passes a window.
- **Boards** [built]: a plank each and a hammer, up to three a side on a door or a window; pulled off, the plank comes back.
- **A new door or window over a broken one** [built]: built whole and new, never repaired.
- **A gate** [built]: a door built over a fence.
- **A fence section** [to build]: built by a player, so a burst fence can be made again (`docs/02`, 2026-10-17).
- **A stone wall** [to build] (#104): a great cost in stone (25% less at Mining 6), a smith's hammer, unbreakable.
- **Remove (X)** [built]: anything a player made or built, with a smith's hammer; refunds nothing [open #104: salvage]. Tearing down the village's own walls [open #104].
- **Every entrance breaks** [built]: a door and a window always have hit points, whatever their wall.
- **A sealed room is a cell** [built]: safe from the dead; left only by the player's own hand (Remove a built wall, or stamp a door).
- **Free building** [built]: a world rule for testing (section 15).
