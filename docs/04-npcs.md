## Immune, and they flee

**Ruled: they flee, and the dead cannot harm them** (immune since 2026-10-28). What is built is the cowering of `43` section 7: one of the dead in sight within a few tiles, a villager cowers about a second, arms up, then runs a few tiles away, stands wary, and walks back to its spot once none is near. A fuller flight is one rule: when a dead thing is in sight, step to the walkable neighbour that most increases distance from the nearest one, preferring tiles toward home if home is known. No weapons, ever, and they never fight for the player; Michael finds that uninteresting and it keeps combat one system. The dead never chase a villager, so none leads a horde to your door.

~~**Follow and stay.**~~ (struck 2026-10-31, `02` "Villagers never follow or stay": "this is a mechanic I have simply changed my mind on"; the paragraph is kept as the record.) Right-click a villager: "Follow me" paths them to stay within a couple of tiles of the player, same loop a chasing zombie uses with a stopping distance; "Stay here" parks them on the tile you point at. That is how you bring a villager home and keep him, and it is the groundwork for everything in `05-someday.md` about vendors and relationships. **With more than one player** (live since 2026-10-13, `31/08`): both are verbs the world performs for whichever player chose them, a guest's sent as a command by the villager's id and run on the host; a follower follows the player who asked, by that player's id, never "the player"; fleeing reads the nearest of the dead to the villager and needs nothing per player.

## Limits, as Michael set them

- No needs, no mood, no goals of their own beyond fleeing, following, and staying. NPCs do not eat or sleep.
- No interaction beyond the active region. A note with `kind: npc` sits at its tile and its route does not tick. Routines resume from the current time of day when the NPC is real again, so nobody is found mid-stride where they could not be.
- On the kept mortal path (`immune: false`, no villager has it now), a dead NPC's turned body is an ordinary zombie record from then on and wanders like one.
- The village has a handful, not a crowd. Six to ten.
