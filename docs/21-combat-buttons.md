## The convention

| Input | Role |
|---|---|
| **Left** | the main hand's primary: swing, stab, or (with a bow) the draw |
| **Right, tap** | the actions menu, opened on release at the point pressed (Zomboid's split) |
| **Right, held** | the off hand's role: **block** with a shield, **brace** with a bow (dual wielding is on the left button, below) |
| **Middle, or `Q`** | the main hand's other mode: the spear's throw (later a halberd's, a hatchet's) |

- **A tap is a press released within 0.2 s of simulation time** (`buttons.js`; counted in steps, so a scripted test sees what a player sees). The menu opens on the release of a tap, at the press position. A tap while a draw is in progress **cancels the draw** instead and opens nothing.
- **The stance is immediate; only the menu waits.** The guard rises and the brace begins on the press, not after 0.2 s, because a defence that arrives late is no defence. A quick tap with a shield up therefore still opens the menu on release; that is the split's price, and Michael's to judge in play. Swinging an off-hand weapon is the exception: it waits for the hold, or every swing would also open the menu.
- **`Q` is the key twin of the middle button**: a trackpad has no middle button. (`R` is taken by rotating a carried or dragged thing.)
- **While the guard is up you can still swing, stab, or throw, for 35 percent of the damage** (Michael, after play; the first build shut attacks while guarded, Valheim's rule, and he overruled it): see the second pass below.

The off-hand swing plays the main arm's animation (rough).

## The guard, second pass (Michael, after "Fixed!!", 2026-10-12)

Ruled: **you can attack from behind the guard, for much less damage; walking with the guard up and with the free draw ("quick shot") is slowed by about 55 percent; protection is total in front and partial at the sides, and the guard must not be a place to live.** Built:

- **How a blow is judged.** A zombie's bite is one event at its attack's active frame (in contact range, line of sight, fixed damage 8), not a hit-test against a body, so the guard scales it by where the attacker stands against the aim, in screen space: **inside the front arc (100 degrees) it does nothing and staggers the attacker; inside the side arc (220 degrees in all) it does half; behind that it lands whole.** A block costs 5 stamina; with less than that the guard is broken (the blow lands whole, the arm is spent, you are winded).
- **No stamina comes back while the guard is up**, so the guard is finite: twenty blocks from full, and every swing from behind it spends stamina too (a stab costs 6). Drop it to recover.
- **Attacking from behind the guard** does 35 percent (`attackMult`): a stab of 30 does 11, a thrown spear 114 does 40. That, with the stamina, is what makes a fight from behind a shield long: ten stabs to a zombie.
- **Walking:** with the guard up 0.45 (was 0.75); the free draw 0.45 (was 0.5); braced 0.2 as before.

What I would watch in play: the stamina bar is now the guard's whole clock; a zombie that hits you from the side every 1.2 s costs 5 stamina and 4 hp, so two or three sides at once end the guard in seconds. All numbers are in the shield's block in `data/items.json`.

## The shield that would not rise with the bag open (Michael, 2026-10-12)

**The real cause, found on his second report ("spawn, take a hatchet and the shield from the chest, equip both, hold right, the shield does not move"):** the guard was gated on the bag and the menu being closed (`!panel.isOpen() && !menu.isMenuOpen()` in `main.js`), and weapons are swapped with the bag open, so every test made while flipping through weapons had the bag open and the guard refused, and a test with it closed worked ("I have seen it work"). **Fix:** the gate is gone. The guard holds with the bag open (the world goes on while you look in it) and with a menu open (a press outside a menu closes it and raises the guard in the same press). Verified with the bag open and with a menu open. Michael's pane had no bow involved; the bow bug below was a real one found while looking, and stays fixed.

### The bow pushing the shield out (found first, a real bug, fixed)

"Holding right to raise the shield with a variety of one-handed weapons: I have seen it work, but after flipping through a bunch of weapons it just stopped for everything; they all swing." **Cause, reproduced:** the bow takes both hands, so equipping it pushes the shield into the bag, and equipping a one-handed weapon afterwards fills only the main hand: the shield stayed in the bag and the right button had nothing to raise (`sword -> sword/shield`, `bow -> bow/bow`, `sword -> sword/` in the console). The guard itself, the menu, the panel, and the right button were all fine through twenty flips by the menu, the panel's DOM, and the belt keys; nothing was wrong with the art. **Fix:** the hands remember. A two-handed item that pushes the off-hand item out remembers it (`player.offMemory`), and when a one-handed item next takes the main hand with the off hand empty, or the two-hander is unequipped or hung on the belt, the remembered item comes back if it is still in the bag or on the belt, once. Something put into the off hand by choice clears the memory. **Also:** the debug corner now ends its hands line with `guard no shield / ready / UP`, so a missing shield is the first thing it shows.

## The guard was hard to read (Michael, after play, 2026-10-12)

"I know what you said it would do but visually it kinda just looks like you are standing there": with the right button held the shield sits at the hand exactly as it did before, so the only evidence of a guard is a blocked blow. Options, cheapest first, none built:

1. **Move the shield, no pose, no re-bake (recommended first).** While `player.blocking`, the renderer draws the held shield where a guard would hold it: in front of the chest toward the aim, a little above the hand, instead of at the hand. A render-only change in the held-item draw (`joints[slot]` replaced for the off slot), sorted in front of the body when the aim is toward the camera and behind when away. It reads as "the shield came up" at a glance and costs nothing on the sheet.
2. **A cue that the guard is up and its arc**: a shield icon over the head like the moodles, or a faint arc on the ground in front of the guard's 150 degrees. The arc is useful (it tells you which attackers are blocked) but is a cousin of the swing wedge Michael had removed (the "semi transparent white cone"), so it should be much fainter and only while guarded, and is his call.
3. **A blocked blow shows**: a spark and a short flash on the shield at the point of contact, the stamina bar pulsing for the 4 it cost, "Blocked" in the speech-bubble style if wanted; a broken guard flashes red. Cheap, and it answers "is this working" at the moment it matters.
4. **A real raised-guard pose** (the off arm up with the shield before the chest): the art pass, and it needs striding legs for walking guarded (the same gap as the drawn bow).

Claude's lean: 1 and 3 together now, 4 with the art, 2 only if the first two do not read.
