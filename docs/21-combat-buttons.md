## The convention

| Input | Role |
|---|---|
| **Left** | the main hand's primary: swing, stab, or (with a bow) the draw |
| **Right, tap** | the actions menu, opened on release at the point pressed (Zomboid's split) |
| **Right, held** | the off hand's role: **block** with a shield, **brace** with a bow (dual wielding is on the left button, below) |
| **Middle, or `Q`** | the main hand's other mode: the spear's throw (later a halberd's, a hatchet's) |

- **A tap is a press released within 0.2 s of simulation time** (`buttons.js`; counted in steps, so a scripted test sees what a player sees). The menu opens on the release of a tap, at the press position. A tap while a draw is in progress **cancels the draw** instead and opens nothing.
- **The stance is immediate; only the menu waits.** The guard rises and the brace begins on the press, not after 0.2 s, because a defence that arrives late is no defence. A quick tap with a shield up therefore still opens the menu on release; that is the split's price, and Michael's to judge in play. Swinging an off-hand weapon is the exception: it waits for the hold, or every swing would also open the menu.
- **`Q` is the key twin of the middle button**: a trackpad has no middle button. (`R` is taken by rotating a carried or dragged thing.)
- **While the guard is up you can still swing, stab, or throw, for 35 percent of the damage** (Michael, after play; the first build shut attacks while guarded, Valheim's rule, and he overruled it): see the second pass below.

## Dual wielding

- **Dual wielding, on the left button** (Michael, 2026-10-12; the first build had the off hand on the held right button, which "never swings", right being the menu): **a sword, hatchet, mace, stick, or torch goes in either hand** (the spear and the halberd never; a shield is off-hand only, so no shield with two weapons). The item menu offers **Equip (main hand)**, which swaps, and **Equip (off hand)**; belt keys draw a weapon into the main hand, so flipping through weapons is not dual wielding by accident. **The left click swings the main hand when it is ready, and the off hand when the main is still recovering**, so clicking faster than a swing's recovery goes back and forth, each hand on its own cooldown and stamina; a short lockout (0.45 of a swing) keeps the two from landing on the same frame. Measured: sword and mace clicked as fast as possible swing every 0.25 s in turn, twice the rate of one weapon; at a pace the main weapon keeps (a click every 0.55 s) the off hand is never used, so **a sword with a torch in the off hand is not dual wielding and costs nothing**. **The price, the glass cannon: an off-hand swing leaves you exposed for 2.5 seconds** (refreshed by each one): a blow takes **1.5 times its damage from the front, 2.5 from the sides, and from behind a hundred times, which is fatal** (an 8-damage bite did 12, 20, and 100 in the test; unexposed, 8). It is state, not loadout, so carrying two weapons or a torch is free and only fighting with both is dangerous; a red bar over the head, above the swing timer's, drains as the exposure runs out. The multiples, the window, and the lockout are `dual` in `data/combat.json`. **The case against the fatal back, offered and for Michael to settle:** it is the only place a single bite from a horde that is already around you ends a run with no counterplay, and a back-turned crowd fight is exactly where dual wielding will be used; `behind: 100` is one number, and 6 would make it two bites. The off-hand swing plays the main arm's animation (rough).

## The guard, second pass (Michael, after "Fixed!!", 2026-10-12)

Ruled: **you can attack from behind the guard, for much less damage; walking with the guard up and with the free draw ("quick shot") is slowed by about 55 percent; protection is total in front and partial at the sides, and the guard must not be a place to live.** Built:

- **How a blow is judged.** A zombie's bite is one event at its attack's active frame (in contact range, line of sight, fixed damage 8), not a hit-test against a body, so the guard scales it by where the attacker stands against the aim, in screen space: **inside the front arc (100 degrees) it does nothing and staggers the attacker; inside the side arc (220 degrees in all) it does half; behind that it lands whole.** A block costs 5 stamina; with less than that the guard is broken (the blow lands whole, the arm is spent, you are winded).
- **No stamina comes back while the guard is up**, so the guard is finite: twenty blocks from full, and every swing from behind it spends stamina too (a stab costs 6). Drop it to recover.
- **Attacking from behind the guard** does 35 percent (`attackMult`): a stab of 30 does 11, a thrown spear 114 does 40. That, with the stamina, is what makes a fight from behind a shield long: ten stabs to a zombie.
- **Walking:** with the guard up 0.45 (was 0.75); the free draw 0.45 (was 0.5); braced 0.2 as before.

What I would watch in play: the stamina bar is now the guard's whole clock; a zombie that hits you from the side every 1.2 s costs 5 stamina and 4 hp, so two or three sides at once end the guard in seconds. All numbers are in the shield's block in `data/items.json`.

## Two rules of the hands

- **Dragging one hand onto the other swaps them** (Michael, 2026-10-12): with a weapon in each hand, dragging either onto the other in the character panel swaps the two (main to off, off to main) when each is allowed in the other's hand; when it cannot (a shield never goes to the main hand), the one in the way goes to the bag as before.
- **The hands remember** (2026-10-12): a two-handed item that pushes the off-hand item out remembers it (`player.offMemory`), and when a one-handed item next takes the main hand with the off hand empty, or the two-hander is unequipped or hung on the belt, the remembered item comes back once if it is still in the bag or on the belt; something put into the off hand by choice clears the memory. The debug corner's hands line ends with `guard no shield / ready / UP`, so a missing shield is the first thing it shows.
