# `src/`: the engine's rules and the code map

Read with the root `CLAUDE.md`. This file says what is, not when; the rulings behind each line are in `docs/02` by date.

## Rules the code keeps

- Tiles are 64×32. Art is drawn at whole pixels in world space; never scale in `drawImage`. Never draw UI into the canvas.
- The world fills the window at one of three zoom levels (`view.js` `ZOOM_LEVELS`), applied by the GPU; the interface has its own scale, capped at 2x. Nothing is laid out against 960×540. DOM over a world point goes through `view.toUi`.
- Nothing materialises in view: no chunk pops in and none of the dead arrive where a player can see (`docs/36`). The active region's boundary stays well beyond the viewport. The one exception is a natural resource's comeback (`docs/46`): an herb or a mushroom may appear in view, and a rock rises out of the ground, an arrival that is seen.
- The map is stored block by block (`map.js`); every tile access goes through `map.tileAt`. A missing chunk is grown on demand: plain floor inside the authored bounds, wild forest with a few of the dead beyond them. **Reading never grows a chunk:** growth is the simulation's ring round each player (`map.ensureAround`, four chunks) and the mutators' (`growTile`); a read beyond the ring sees the frozen blank tile; the renderer, the ground, the light, and sight never change the world, and the ground bakes a chunk only once its neighbours are grown (`check("growth")`).
- **The map is generated, never typed:** `python -I tools/mapgen/valley.py` writes `data/maps/valley.json` from a description per place. A floor definition's `walk` (false, or a speed factor) and `drink`, and a wall definition's `wall.seeThrough`, are how water, shallow water, fences, bars, and arrow slits work; nothing in code names them. A see-through wall bounds no room. A building's `wall` may be per side, `partitions` make rooms inside it, a `fence` feature walls a rect with gates (on some `sides` only, if it says); a water floor rect's `group` names the water ("river", "pond"; `map.waterBodyAt`, and `data/fishing.json` has a table for each name, which the validator enforces); an opening may be authored `open`, `broken`, or boarded (`boards: { in, out }`); a placeholder box definition carries a `label`, drawn on the box.
- **A change to anything on a chunk says so:** what a guest must see marks the chunk (`map.touch`, `markDirty`, or a mutator that calls them); what only the save keeps (a fuel between units, a timer, a stamp, health short of a break, a bag made on first use) calls `map.unsaved`. The save keeps each chunk's form until one of them drops it (`game.serialize`); `game.verifySaveCache` catches a miss (the server's log says THE SAVE'S CACHE MISSED); `node tools/savecache.js` proves the list.
- Rooms are found by flood fill, never authored. Walls are state on tile edges (`n` and `w` slots, Zomboid style), never objects; a door or a window is a wall with a kind and a state.
- The ground is one baked picture per chunk (`ground.js`, `docs/15`), drawn as one quad; nothing draws the floor per tile and nothing on the ground plane alternates by tile parity. A floor changes only through `map.setFloor`.
- Light is one function (`light.js`). Nothing else computes light. Light feeds sight. In the renderer every vertex carries the world point its light is read from; nothing is lit in screen space.
- The sway: the poke's stamp and the renderer's lean read the same clock, `loop.simTime()`; a wall-clock read on either side switches every wobble off. A sprite definition turns the sway off with `"sway": false` or replaces it with a `clickAnim` (the well).
- Drawn art comes in through sprite definitions (`data/sprites/*.json`, read by `assets.js`; `docs/17`): packed `images` with top-vertex and per-frame anchors, `drawings` with mirrors for facings, two-tile furniture sliced per tile along a vertical screen line; a crop's rows follow the furrows of its soil.
- The sim rolls only through `rng.js` (seeded from the map) and reads the clock only through `loop.simTime()`; a new `Math.random` or `performance.now` in a sim file is a bug. `game.js` reads no window and no DOM: the whole world runs headless under `tools/world.js` and hashes the same.
- **The draw is between steps** (`interp.js`, #161): the world steps at sixty a second, the screen draws as often as the display asks, and everything that moves is drawn blended by how far between steps the frame falls (one step, 16 ms, behind at most). A new thing that moves on the sim's step and is drawn from its `gx`, `gy` is in `interp.mark`'s list or it will judder on a 144 Hz display.
- Per-player state lives on the player, never in a module-level variable; the world holds a list of players and the dead hunt the nearest they see.

## The world and its state

- `game.js`: the world's state, `create` from a map file, the step order (`begin`, `step`), `serialize` and `restore` (the save and the join's format), `hashText`, `syncParts` (what `check("sync")` compares), `verifySaveCache`.
- `map.js`: tiles, edge walls, collision, rooms, authoring (sets, features, scatter, wild), `propById`, `feedFire`, `setFireLit` and `setLantern` (a switch or a lantern on or off; a put-out emits `firePutOut`, never the burnt-out `fireOut`), growth and eviction (`evictFar`; `dropChunk` the one way a chunk leaves memory, the host's eviction and a guest told of one alike; `takeEvicted` the dropped chunks for the frame).
- `world.js`: the world's time scale (1 awake, `sleepTimeScale` from `data/tuning.json` while every living player sleeps; `game.timeScaleNow`) and the id counter every prop and entry takes its instance id from.
- `interp.js`: drawn positions between steps (#161): each step marks where every character and the camera stood (`mark`), and `render` puts them `loop.alpha()` of the way to where the step left them (`apply`) and takes it off again in a `finally` (`restore`); nothing in the simulation or the interface logic ever reads a blended position; a jump over `feel.blendJump` tiles is not blended; arrows and the fireball are marked too, with their height.
- `visibility.js` line of sight · `sight.js` how much of the dead in view a player sees in the dark (the client's own, from its own light; `docs/07`) · `light.js` the one light function, its per-chunk cache, portals · `clock.js` the day · `rules.js` the sim's rules (a snapshot of the settings for a browser's world, the save's for a server world; carried in the save since version 2) · `tuning.js` the entities' numbers from `data/tuning.json` · `rng.js` the one seeded generator · `events.js` the event bus · `iso.js` the projection · `pathfind.js`.
- `reach.js`: the one reach test for every player action (#208): distance and a clear line of sight to the nearest tile (`reachProblem`, `canReach`); a wall or a shut door or shutter stops it, a fence or an open window does not (a sealed room is a cell, #147). The menu's actions, a craft's station and ingredients, the panels' closing, a poke, the bed, the fire, and the chop ask it (the farming, fruit, mining, and fishing verbs keep their own distance-and-line tests, which agree with it).
- `verbs.js`: the verb table; `verbs.run(name, world, player, target, args)` with targets as addresses; rows are defined where they belong (`sleep` in `entities/player.js`, `craft` in `stations.js`, `dip` in `burn.js`).
- `bucket.js`: the bucket item (`vessel` in its definition, its water the entry's `fill`): the verbs `fill` (at water) and `pour` (on a plot, 3 by 3 through `farming.soak`) and the menu's entries for a tile, #199.
- `water.js`: water, tainted and clean (`docs/44`): no fixed source is clean (`tuning.water.clean`, empty), an entry's water tainted unless it is `clean`, `mixInto` (a fill from a tainted source taints it all; the kit's skin starts clean), and the `boil` verb at a lit cooking fire (a fire that `cooks`) by the fire's prop id and the skin's uid: `args.timed` starts the bar (`boilAction`, `boilSeconds`, `boilReach`), whose end runs the verb again and sets `clean`, the fill kept; the three ways in are the Use row (`data/interactions.json`), the skin's menu in the bag and on the belt, and the fire's menu (the fullest tainted skin), each greyed with why. Skins only: a bucket's boil waits for fluid transfer. `sick.js`: Sick, tainted water's debuff (`player.sick = { stacks, left }` as `player.bleed`, the host's step runs it down, `left` to two decimals on the wire): tiredness `sickTiredMult` times as fast, food `sickHealMult` of its heal, `sickDays` a stack up to `sickMaxStacks`; `taintedDrink` is the one roll both ways to drink tainted water make (a source's Drink, `player.drink`): Sick at `sickChance` (once Sick, every tainted drink a stack), the infection at `infectionChance`, two floats always.

## Rendering and the figure

## Multiplayer

- `netsmooth.js`: remote bodies on a guest drawn between the host's frames (#248): the last few positions with the host's sim time, drawn `feel.netDelaySteps` steps behind the newest frame, interpolated; `netTo` stays the host's truth. `tools/netsmooth.js` measures it against the old walk-to-each-frame.
- `net.js` the wire (`docs/31-multiplayer/04` status, `09`): the join a load, then frames every third step at normal speed (`FRAME_EVERY`); a player's record in light and heavy parts, the dead, the villagers, the corpses, and the animals (a lean record of their own, `animalRecord`; the homes' renewal in `animalHomes` when it changes) as deltas with a roster when membership changes; the chunks the host dropped (`evicted`, a guest drops them too; a guest back from a step away keeps its away screen, and predicts nothing, until a frame carries its player and the ring round it, `returning`); a guest's own player runs the controller in predict mode and the hit comes from the host; a guest's inputs as runs while the world runs fast; `PROTOCOL` (11), the browser id in the join, the owner key (`setOwnerKey`), the host secret (`setHostSecret`), `setSyncReports` and `setTestSnapshot` for `check("sync")`, `status().wire` for a frame's bytes. The default wire is the Fly relay (`RELAY_URL`, `wss://cmz-relay.fly.dev`); `?relay=none` is the loopback of one browser's tabs, `?relay=ws://localhost:8012` a local relay.
- `host.js` the authority's half, shared by the browser host and the server world: joins and whose character is whose (a join over the cap, `playerCapBrowser` or `playerCapServer` in `data/tuning.json`, is refused with `full` before anything is made or taken from the save), dormancy (a guest away sixty seconds of game time goes dormant into the save and comes back on the rejoin), stepping away (a player idle five minutes in a shared world), the world's events answered for the players they are for, the routing of a message or a log line to the acting guest; `MAX_OWNERS`.
- `save.js` the worlds store (IndexedDB `cmz`/`worlds`, the index in localStorage; `VERSION` 3 with `save.upgrade` the migrations; an autosave every 60 s of game time) · `identities.js` identities (a name and a look), the browser id, the friends' worlds bookmarks, the owned keys · `loop.js` the fixed step, hit-stop, `advance`, the sim clock, the pause count, and `setBackgroundDriver` (a Worker's tick steps a world with friends in it while the tab is hidden; a solo world stays stopped).
